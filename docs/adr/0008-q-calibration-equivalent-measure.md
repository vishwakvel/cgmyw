# Q calibration: solve M from the martingale condition; pin Yn, Yp (and, under strict equivalence, Cn, Cp)

Status: accepted (2026-10-18); default Q search revised to C_FREE after Dr. Madan's answers on 2026-10-19 (see the end of this ADR).

**Pinning split (8 params).** Madan & Wang, "Asset Pricing, Measure Changes, and a New Lévy Model" (2026), Section 2: equivalence (finite Hellinger distance) forbids changing C and Y on either side. Cn, Cp, Yn, Yp are pinned at their P values; G, M, Wn, Wp move.

**Martingale condition, not a drift correction.** The paper imposes `psi_Q(-i) = 0` (its r=q=0 simplification) as a constraint on the Q parameters. We use the market forward instead: `T * psi_Q(-i) = ln(F/S0)`. There is no free drift: `psi_cgmyw`'s closed form carries the drift `m(theta)` (analytic continuation of the mean of k) for every Y in (0,2), and equivalence moves the drift by exactly `m(theta_Q) - m(theta_P)`, so the Q exponent is `psi_cgmyw` at the Q parameters, nothing added. Since `psi(-i)` is strictly decreasing in M, M is solved by a 1-D root-find (`qfit.solve_m`) rather than a constrained optimizer: every point the optimizer visits is an equivalent martingale measure. The Carr-Madan pricer still applies `omega = -psi(-i)`; under the constraint this is the identity up to solver round-off.

**Time units.** P is fit on daily returns, so Cn, Cp are per trading day. G, M, Y, W describe jump sizes and are time-free; only arrival rates scale: `C_annual = 252 * C_daily`, giving `psi_annual = 252 * psi_daily` exactly. Option maturity is calendar days / 365.

**Objective.** Mean squared vega-weighted price error `((model - mid)/vega)^2`, vega at the market IV, per the meeting (no Black-Scholes inversion per evaluation). IV RMSE is reported alongside it.

**Validation.** From the paper's Table 1 P-parameters, `qfit.risk_neutralize` reproduces its Table 2 risk-neutral (G, M, Wp, Wn) for all ten tickers to about 3 significant figures (the published points leave ~1e-5 residual drift, i.e. solver tolerance). An unrestricted CGMYW fit (all of Cn, Cp, Yn, Yp freed too) reproduces AAPL smiles to 0.1-0.4 vol points, so the pricer and optimizer are not the bottleneck.

**Finding: the equivalent Q measure cannot fit AAPL's skew, and this is not a code defect.** IV RMSE with Cn, Cp, Yn, Yp pinned: 6.4 vol points on 2023-01-13, 8.7 on 2025-08-29. Unrestricted CGMYW fits the same smiles to 0.4 and 0.14. Diagnosis (2026-10-18):

1. *Martingale lock.* With no diffusion, equivalence also fixes the drift, so the martingale condition is a constraint on the jump parameters alone. Each tail's contribution to psi(-i) is of order C*Gamma(1-Y)*G^(Y-1) (the mean jump rate), far larger than ln(F/S0)/T, so the condition forces the down and up contributions to nearly cancel: M tracks G (scan on 2025-08-29: M ~ 1.3 G at every G), and at a realistic ATM vol the attainable put skew is ~2 vol points against the market's 17.5. Freeing the drift alone (C, Y still pinned) cuts 2025-08-29 from 9.5 to 1.8 vol points, M running to its bound -- but CGMYW is pure-jump by design, so there is no Brownian part to carry a free drift, and that route is out.
2. *P and Q want incompatible (C, Y).* Refitting P with C, Y fixed at the option-implied values raises tail-probability RMSE 0.008 -> 0.049 (6x). A joint fit with shared (C, Y) has no interior compromise: it lands either at P-good / Q 8.6 vol points or at Q-good / P 25x worse.
3. *(C, Y) are weakly identified from 251 daily returns.* Different windows land in different regimes (Y ~ 1.67 symmetric on 2023-01-13; Yn ~ 0.15 / Yp ~ 0.86 on 2025-08-29) at similar objective. Since these are exactly the pinned parameters, P-fit identification drives Q.

**What the source papers settle (2026-10-19).**
- *Drift.* The CGMYW draft says the time-series model "will have a drift which is then risk-neutralized", and Madan & Wang's *Multi-Asset Markovian Economies* (eq. 25-26) writes the martingale condition as physical drift + psi_Q(-i) = 0. Tested: adding a drift to the P-fit leaves Q unchanged (2025-08-29: estimated drift ~0, still 8.7 vol points; 2023-01-13: 6.4 -> 6.25 with an implausible -1.3%/day drift). Not the bottleneck; not yet added to `pfit`.
- *Relaxing equivalence.* The Markovian paper (Sec. 3) already lets Cn, Cp change when G, M alone cannot meet the conditions, and shows its required-return formula (eq. 36) survives without equivalence. Tested on AAPL with Yn, Yp still pinned and Cn, Cp, G, Wn, Wp free (M from the martingale condition): IV RMSE 2.8 / 2.5 vol points with the 252-day P window, 0.64 / 0.75 with a 1033-day window (2025-08-29 / 2023-01-13). Risk-neutral C comes out below physical C on both sides.
- *Window.* The paper estimated on a fixed 1033-day window. On AAPL, 1033 days gives steadier P estimates (Y ~ 1.0-1.5 on both dates) than 252 days. A longer window alone does not rescue strict pinning, though (9.4 / 2.4 vol points).
- *Martingale target and units.* The paper's r=q=0 is stated as preliminary, so the forward-based target stands. The 252x scaling of C is forced by psi being linear in C.

**Resolved with Dr. Madan, 2026-10-19.**
1. *Q step:* "keep it free for performance accuracy." The default Q fit is now `C_FREE`: Cn, Cp, G, Wn, Wp searched, Yn, Yp pinned at P, M from the martingale condition. This is his Markovian-paper relaxation. It is not strictly equivalent, so required returns come from eq. 36's kernel difference. `PINNED` (strictly equivalent) and `UNRESTRICTED` are still reported for comparison.
2. *Window:* 1033 days estimates better, but traders don't use that many years. The 252-day rolling window stays (ADR-0003), and differing from the paper's window is fine.
3. *Weighting:* AD weights are the recommendation, and he used them too: uniform weights would fit only the large central probabilities. For tail probabilities p never approaches 1, so only p -> 0 matters in 1/(p(1-p)); the (1-p) factor only bites when fitting plain CDF values. The gap to his Table 1 was therefore the threshold grid, not the weighting. With AD weights and a grid spanning the whole range (as in his Figure 1), AAPL's 1033-day fit reaches a full-range RMSE of 0.0040, against 0.0047 for his Table 1 values and 0.023 for our old tails-only grid. `pfit.DEFAULT_THRESHOLD_QUANTILES` is now 15 quantiles per side (1-45%, 55-99%).

*Results after both changes (AAPL, 252-day window):* the P-fit lands in the paper's regime (Y ~ 0.04-0.06, Wn ~ 15 vs Wp ~ 5) on both dates. IV RMSE in vol points, 2023-01-13 / 2025-08-29: main `C_FREE` 0.97 / 0.74; strictly equivalent 6.3 / 9.0; martingale-only 6.2 / 7.7; unrestricted 0.42 / 0.14. On 2025-08-29 the main fit drives G to ~0.04 and leaves the down tail to the power tilt (Wn ~ 11) -- the power tilt doing the work the model was built for.

Consequence: `qfit.calibrate_q` defaults to `search=C_FREE`; `PINNED` and `UNRESTRICTED` remain available and are labelled as comparisons wherever they are reported.
